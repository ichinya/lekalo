Implemented on `ichinya/m7-issue-35`, based on `ichinya/M7` at `a56ee5786f1724f9ee61ef973088c7679684c14d`, following the approved [research](issue-35-research.md). Lekalo-side implementation is complete: the pure neutral assessment service, CLI/process contract, registered diagnostics, schemas, goldens, provenance and CI gates are supplied. **Real HLV end-to-end execution and Extension verify/done aggregation remain external acceptance work.** No real HLV executable was installed or invoked, and no AIFHub checkout or other worktree was accessed or modified during this implementation phase. Synthetic receipts are identified as such in fixtures, gate output and the normative [assessment contract documentation](../trace-assessment.md).
